To be or not to be a question we can recognize right away as one of William Shakespeare's classic lines.
According to tradition the great poet and English dramatist William Shakespeare was born in Stratford-upon-Avon on the 23rd of April 1564.
Now though it is impossible to be certain of the exact day he was born, church records show that he was baptized on the 26th of April and three days were a customary amount of time before baptizing a newborn.
But to this day his timeless works are still relevant in today's changing world.
